---
title: "Welcome to lsext.org"
description: "Dual-hosted , educate retrocomputing resource for vintage SunOS & Solaris systems."
---

Our mission is to preserve,educate and keep SPARC workstations usefull now and in the future
 for those afortunate to have access to one via hardware or emulation.

For this we are porting software never before available to the platform
in an effort to preserve them for the future.

We are currently focusing in 2 different Sparc OS millestones. 

- SunOS 4.x   
4.1.4 Marks the end of an era, Early SunOS releases were based on BSDUnix
(Berkley Unix). Older Sparcs match perfectly with it they are snappy, responsive
and need little ram.  

- Solaris 2.5.1   
Is the last stable SRV4 (ATT Unix). before Sun big push for 64-bit and all the 
bloat that came with it.
